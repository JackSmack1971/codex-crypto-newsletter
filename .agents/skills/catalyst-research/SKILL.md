# Catalyst Research

## Inputs and outputs

Input: project/event claim, asset identity, target horizon, and permitted sources. Output: catalyst chronology with verified status, dates/timezone, primary evidence, likely relevance, and uncertainty.

## Workflow

1. Locate primary project or venue evidence where authorized. 2. Verify event status and distinguish announced, scheduled, completed, delayed, and rumored. 3. Normalize dates/timezones and identify recycled claims. 4. Assess token linkage and plausible transmission mechanism. 5. Record what would confirm or invalidate the catalyst.

## Evidence and failure behavior

Evidence: dated source links, retrieved/observed timestamps, status rationale, token linkage, and contrary evidence. Failure: `UNVERIFIED` if only secondary rumor exists; do not invent timing or imply price impact.

Use the shared [research evidence contract](../crypto-daily/references/research-evidence.md) for source permissions, evidence labels, and common failure handling.
