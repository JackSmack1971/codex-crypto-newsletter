# Fixed editorial contract

Concise readable front end; extended scorecards, formulas, desk outputs, manifests/exclusions in a clearly separated RESEARCH APPENDIX or actual companion artifact. Never invent an artifact or hide a blocker for brevity. Sharp, skeptical, quantitative, readable; no hype, guarantees, false precision/independence or recycled catalysts. Put primary-source links near claims when permitted. Label contentious FACT / CALCULATION / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS.

Use exactly this section order:

1. SMALL-CAP CRYPTO DAILY — date, UTC/local cutoff, tool-policy status, publication state, regime, PICK / NO PICK.
2. MARKET IN 90 SECONDS — measured direction/breadth, cross-asset tension, catalyst calendar, scoped confidence.
3. MARKET DASHBOARD — BTC/ETH, sample coverage, advancers/decliners, median 7D, dispersion, realized volatility/correlation, dominance, positioning; missing fields explicit.
4. WHAT'S MOVING — verified relative leaders and laggards; separate price action from corroborated flows.
5. UNDER THE RADAR — genuinely new primary-source events, recycled headlines, uncertain status, unlock conflicts, identity pitfalls.
6. TODAY'S DEEP DIVE — strongest research hypothesis even on no-pick days; token-specific economics, upside/downside, 7–21-day timing, counterevidence, next verification.
7. SMALL-CAP LEADERBOARD — current tier, complete signal grades only, coverage, confidence, gate/reasons, instrument-backed shorts/hedges. Full funnel in appendix.
8. PICK OF THE DAY — exactly one MANDATE-COMPLIANT RESEARCH PICK only after all applicable gates PASS and explicit genuine independent reviewer PASS; otherwise NO PICK — WATCH/CASH and the single most important missing approval condition. Never imply trade or investment-committee authorization.
9. RISK DESK — competing scenarios, execution, security, supply, macro, concentration. Observation thresholds are not live stop orders.
10. PREVIOUS PICK — ACCOUNTABILITY — original persisted decision and available outcomes or NOT REPORTABLE: ORIGINAL RECORD UNAVAILABLE.
11. WATCHLIST — validation milestones, exact trigger/expiration where supported, explicit names to avoid.
12. BOTTOM LINE — conditional what changed, what invalidates, what to monitor.
13. SOURCES, METHOD AND RESEARCH APPENDIX — authorized sources/times/gaps, counts, nine desk statuses, finalist eight-gate tables, reviewer verdicts, score evidence, persistence status, machine-readable records if storage unavailable.
